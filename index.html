Who Are We?

The International Union Council (IUC) is an international organization established to bring nations together through diplomacy, cooperation, and collective governance. It provides a common institutional framework in which member states can discuss international affairs, coordinate policies, resolve disputes, and pursue shared objectives.

Inspired by the principles of international cooperation embodied by the United Nations, the IUC facilitates dialogue between nations and provides institutions through which international decisions can be developed and implemented.
